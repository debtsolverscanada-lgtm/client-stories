Privacy Policy

DebtSolvers Canada · Last updated November 3, 2026

DebtSolvers Canada ("we", "us") helps Canadians understand their options for dealing with high-interest debt. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address and province.
  • Information about your debts and finances that you share with us so we can review your options, such as who you owe, balances, monthly payments and income.
  • Records of our conversations with you, including calls, text messages and emails. Calls may be recorded for quality and training.
  • Basic website and ad data, such as which ad or page brought you to us, collected by advertising platforms like Meta and Google.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the consultation you asked for and answer your questions.
  • To review your situation and explain the debt-relief options that may fit you.
  • If you choose to go ahead, to prepare your file and share it with the Licensed Insolvency Trustee or other professional who handles your case.
  • To keep records, meet our legal obligations and improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • A Licensed Insolvency Trustee or other professional, only when you ask us to move forward with your file.
  • Service providers that help us run our business (for example phone, email, CRM and document-signing tools). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Texts, calls and emails

When you submit a form, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text or email about your inquiry. You can stop at any time: reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or tell us on a call. Message and data rates may apply.

How we protect it

Access to your information is limited to staff who need it to help you. We use reputable, secure service providers and keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to further contact. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer help with your file.

Cookies and ads

This page does not run its own tracking scripts. The advertising platforms that showed you our ad (such as Meta and Google) may use cookies under their own privacy policies. You can manage ad preferences in your Meta and Google account settings.

Questions or requests?
Email admin@debtsolver.ca or call 437-836-9854.

DebtSolvers Canada is not a Licensed Insolvency Trustee. Consumer proposals are filed and administered by a Licensed Insolvency Trustee.
